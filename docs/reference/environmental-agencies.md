# Regulatory References

## About BioRemPP Database

BioRemPP provides a comprehensive database that includes compounds identified as **priority environmental pollutants**. These compounds are recognized by global regulatory agencies or entities due to their environmental and human health impacts.

Our database integrates information from multiple authoritative sources to ensure comprehensive coverage of priority pollutants across different regulatory frameworks and geographical regions.

!!! info "Last Updated"
    All regulatory references were last reviewed and updated on **December, 2025**.

---

## Priority Environmental Pollutants

Priority pollutants are compounds that pose significant risks to environmental and human health. These substances are targeted for monitoring and remediation efforts due to their:

### Key Characteristics

**Persistence**
:   Resistance to degradation in the environment

**Toxicity**
:   Harmful effects on living organisms and ecosystems

**Bioaccumulation**
:   Tendency to accumulate in organisms and food chains

**Mobility**
:   Capacity to spread through environmental media

!!! success "BioRemPP's Mission"
    By focusing on these compounds, BioRemPP aims to support global initiatives for environmental sustainability and the development of innovative bioremediation strategies.

---

## Common Categories of Priority Pollutants

### :material-oil:{ .organic } Organic Compounds

Persistent organic pollutants (POPs) including polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) and polychlorinated biphenyls (PCBs).

**Examples:**

- Benzene, Toluene, Xylene (BTX)
- Polycyclic Aromatic Hydrocarbons
- Chlorinated Solvents
- Petroleum Hydrocarbons

### :material-atom:{ .heavy-metals } Heavy Metals

Toxic metallic elements that persist in the environment and accumulate in living organisms.

**Examples:**

- Lead (Pb)
- Mercury (Hg)
- Cadmium (Cd)
- Chromium (Cr)

### :material-sprout:{ .pesticides } Pesticides

Agricultural and industrial chemicals designed to control pests but with environmental impacts.

**Examples:**

- Organophosphates
- Organochlorines
- Herbicides
- Fungicides

### :material-factory:{ .industrial } Industrial Chemicals

Synthetic compounds used in manufacturing processes with potential environmental hazards.

**Examples:**

- Phthalates
- Dioxins and Furans
- Brominated Flame Retardants
- Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances (PFAS)

## Key Environmental Regulatory Agencies

BioRemPP integrates priority pollutant lists from leading international regulatory agencies and environmental frameworks:

### :flag_us: Agency for Toxic Substances and Disease Registry (ATSDR)

**Country:** USA

The ATSDR identifies hazardous substances and assesses their health effects. Its priority substance list guides environmental cleanup and regulatory actions in the USA.

**Key Features:**

- Comprehensive toxicological profiles
- Prioritization based on health risks
- Guidance for environmental cleanup
- Support for regulatory decisions

[:octicons-link-external-16: Visit ATSDR](https://www.atsdr.cdc.gov/programs/substance-priority-list.html){ .md-button }

---

### :flag_br: National Environmental Council (CONAMA)

**Country:** Brazil

CONAMA establishes guidelines for pollution control, water quality, and the regulation of hazardous substances in Brazil. It prioritizes pollutants based on environmental impact and public health risks.

**Key Features:**

- Brazilian environmental standards
- Water and soil quality guidelines
- Hazardous substance regulations
- Regional adaptation focus

[:octicons-link-external-16: Visit CONAMA](https://conama.mma.gov.br/){ .md-button }

---

### :flag_us: Environmental Protection Agency (US EPA)

**Country:** USA

The EPA regulates pollutants through programs like the National Priority List and CERCLA (Superfund), focusing on mitigating environmental contamination and promoting sustainability.

**Key Features:**

- Superfund site management
- Priority pollutant lists
- Cleanup standards and protocols
- Risk assessment frameworks

[:octicons-link-external-16: Visit EPA NPL](https://www.epa.gov/superfund/superfund-national-priorities-list-npl){ .md-button }

---

### :flag_eu: European Parliament (EP)

**Country:** European Union

The EP enforces directives like the REACH regulation and the Water Framework Directive, which aim to protect the environment and human health by monitoring and regulating pollutants across Europe.

**Key Features:**

- REACH chemical regulation
- Water Framework Directive
- Harmonized EU standards
- Precautionary principle application

[:octicons-link-external-16: Visit EU WFD](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2000/60/oj){ .md-button }

---

### :globe_with_meridians: International Agency for Research on Cancer (IARC)

**Country:** International (WHO)

The IARC classifies compounds based on their carcinogenic potential (Groups 1, 2A, and 2B). This classification informs regulatory actions and prioritization for monitoring.

**Key Features:**

- Carcinogenicity assessment
- Evidence-based classification
- Global reference standard
- Monograph publications

[:octicons-link-external-16: Visit IARC Classifications](https://monographs.iarc.who.int/list-of-classifications/){ .md-button }

---

### :flag_ca: Canadian Environmental Protection Act (CEPA)

**Country:** Canada

The CEPA prioritizes substances based on their toxicity, persistence, and bioaccumulation. PSL1 and PSL2 guide environmental management actions in Canada.

**Key Features:**

- Priority Substances Lists (PSL1 & PSL2)
- Toxicity screening framework
- Bioaccumulation assessment
- Canadian-specific risk evaluation

[:octicons-link-external-16: Visit CEPA Registry](https://www.canada.ca/en/environment-climate-change/services/canadian-environmental-protection-act-registry/substances-list){ .md-button }

## Water Framework Directive (WFD)

The **Water Framework Directive (2000/60/EC)** is a key European Union directive that aims to achieve good water quality in surface and groundwater across Europe.

### Key Objectives

- **Prevent deterioration** of aquatic ecosystems
- **Promote sustainable water use**
- **Reduce pollution** of priority substances
- **Ensure progressive reduction** of priority hazardous substances

The WFD provides a comprehensive framework for monitoring and remediation efforts, targeting priority pollutants that pose risks to aquatic environments.

!!! note "European Integration"
    The WFD represents one of the most comprehensive water protection frameworks globally, integrating ecological, chemical, and quantitative status assessment.

## IARC Carcinogenicity Classifications

The **International Agency for Research on Cancer (IARC)** classifies compounds into groups based on the strength of evidence for their carcinogenic potential:

### Group 1: Carcinogenic to Humans

!!! danger "Group 1"
    **Carcinogenic to humans**
    
    Sufficient evidence of carcinogenicity in humans.

**Examples:** Benzene, Formaldehyde, Asbestos, Vinyl Chloride

---

### Group 2A: Probably Carcinogenic to Humans

!!! warning "Group 2A"
    **Probably carcinogenic to humans**
    
    Limited evidence in humans, sufficient evidence in experimental animals.

**Examples:** Polychlorinated biphenyls (PCBs), Creosotes, some Nitrosamines

---

### Group 2B: Possibly Carcinogenic to Humans

!!! info "Group 2B"
    **Possibly carcinogenic to humans**
    
    Limited evidence in humans, less than sufficient evidence in experimental animals.

**Examples:** Chloroform, Naphthalene, some Pesticides
